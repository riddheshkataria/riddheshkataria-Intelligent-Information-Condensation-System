# Sprint Review and Team Performance - Q2 Retrospective

## Overview
As we wrap up Q2, this review covers the achievements, challenges, and overall performance of the cross-functional teams.

## Highlights
- Successfully shipped the automated document summarization feature ahead of schedule.
- Reduced overall technical debt by resolving 45 legacy bug tickets.
- High team morale reported in the quarterly pulse survey (8.5/10).

## Areas for Improvement
- **Communication Bottlenecks**: Instances where the frontend team was blocked due to delayed API specification approvals. We need stricter enforcement of API contracts before sprints begin.
- **QA Environments**: Staging environments were occasionally unstable, causing delays in integration testing. We must allocate time next quarter to stabilize our CI/CD pipelines.

## Next Steps
- Managers to conduct 1-on-1s next week to discuss individual performance goals.
- DevOps team to prioritize stabilizing the staging deployment flow.
- Organize a team-building offsite to celebrate Q2 wins.
