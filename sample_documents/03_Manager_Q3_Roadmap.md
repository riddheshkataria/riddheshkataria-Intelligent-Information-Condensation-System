# Q3 Project Roadmap & Resource Allocation

## Executive Summary
This document serves as the high-level roadmap for the third quarter. Focus will be directed towards optimizing our core application performance and rolling out the new analytics dashboard to our enterprise clients.

## Key Objectives
- Launch 'Intelligent Information Condensation System' beta to select partners.
- Reduce customer onboarding time by 30%.
- Ensure a 99.99% uptime for API services.

## Resource Allocation
- **Engineering**: 60% of engineering bandwidth will be dedicated to infrastructure upgrades (API v2). 40% will be allocated to new feature development.
- **Design/UX**: Focus entirely on the self-serve onboarding portal.
- **QA/Testing**: Integration testing for the new ML pipeline.

## Budget Overview
- Increased cloud computational budget by $20,000 for training LLM models.
- Approved two new hires for the Data Science team.

## Meeting Cadence
- Weekly syncs on Mondays at 10 AM.
- Monthly all-hands to review target metrics and KPIs.
